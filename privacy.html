Privacy Policy

Last updated: 31 August 2026

In short: This app does not collect, store, or share any personal data. There are no accounts, no tracking, and no advertising.

1. Who we are

This app ("German Vocabulary in Context — C1") is developed and published by Ziya Ibrahimov, trading as Ziya Learning Apps, Rothenfelder Str. 39, 38440 Wolfsburg, Germany.

2. What data we collect

None. The app runs entirely on your device. It has no user accounts, no sign-in, and no server backend. We do not collect names, email addresses, location, contacts, or any other personal information.

3. How the app works

  • All learning content (words, texts, dialogues, exercises and translations) is bundled inside the app and works fully offline.
  • Spoken German audio is produced on your device using your system's text-to-speech voices. No audio is recorded or transmitted.
  • Your language choice and progress within a session stay on your device and are not sent anywhere.

4. Analytics, advertising and tracking

The app contains no analytics, no advertising SDKs, and no third-party trackers. No usage data leaves your device.

5. Permissions

The app does not request access to your camera, microphone, location, contacts, or storage. It only uses your device's built-in text-to-speech to read German aloud.

6. Children's privacy

Because the app collects no data at all, it is safe for users of all ages, including children.

7. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published here with a new "last updated" date.
